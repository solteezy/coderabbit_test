What is a Lightbulb?

A lightbulb is an electric device that produces light by heating a filament or by passing an electric current through a gas or semiconductor.

Key Components
  • • Glass bulb enclosure
  • • Light-emitting element (filament, gas, or LED)
  • • Base for electrical connection
  • • Support wires or contacts
Basic Function

When electricity flows through the lightbulb, it either heats the filament to produce light (incandescent) or excites the gas/semiconductor to emit photons (LED/CFL). The glass bulb protects the light-emitting element and prevents oxygen from reaching it.
